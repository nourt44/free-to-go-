Our mission

Our Mission Is Simple

To help create a more accessible and inclusive community where wheelchair users can explore Amman with greater independence and confidence.

Our Goals

  • Raise awareness about accessibility.
  • Help wheelchair users discover suitable places.
  • Encourage businesses to improve accessibility.
  • Promote equality and inclusion.
  • Use technology to make a positive difference.
Our impact
A More Accessible Community Starts With Us.

We believe accessibility is not a luxury. It is something everyone deserves.

Through Free To Go, we hope to make exploring public places easier, encourage businesses to become more accessible, and inspire people to create positive changes in their communities.
